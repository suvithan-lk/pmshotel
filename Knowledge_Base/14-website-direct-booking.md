# Website and Direct Booking

## Website scope

Initial release includes: - Rebranded responsive website - Editable
content - Offers - Contact - Analytics - Direct booking

## Direct booking principles

The booking journey must be mobile friendly and expose: - Dates -
Occupancy - Complete price - Policy - Inclusions - Quote expiry

Confirmation must revalidate the commercial terms and inventory. The
platform must never silently substitute dates, room type or rate.

## Analytics

Website analytics is consent-aware. Operational secrets must not be
exposed to analytics events.

## Localization

The initial operating language is English. Additional languages can be
prioritized after content approval.
