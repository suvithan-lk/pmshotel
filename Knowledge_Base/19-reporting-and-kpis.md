# Reporting and KPIs

## Operational reports

### Daily manager

Occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, arrivals, departures, revenue, tenders and
exceptions. Must reconcile to closed business-date transactions.

### Reservation production

Booked rooms and revenue by stay date, source, channel and status.
Cancellations and no-shows shown separately.

### Front desk

Expected, arrived, in-house, due-out, checked-out and unresolved
balances. Drill to reservation/stay.

### Room status

Occupancy, housekeeping, maintenance and readiness by room.

### Housekeeping

Assigned, started, cleaned, inspected, rework and duration. Show owner
and last update and apply DND/paused-time rules.

### Folio/payment

Charges, deposits, payments, refunds, balances and aging.

### Restaurant

Net sales, tax, service charge, discounts, voids, tenders and average
check. Reconcile to POS close and room charges.

### Channel reconciliation

New, modified, cancelled, mapping, delivery and stale exceptions. Every
exception has owner/status.

### Audit exceptions

Overrides, refunds, reversals, voids, access changes and exports. Drill
to immutable entries.

## KPI definitions

-   Occupancy % = occupied room nights / available room nights
-   ADR = accommodation room revenue / sold room nights
-   RevPAR = accommodation room revenue / available room nights
-   Direct booking share = confirmed direct room nights / total
    confirmed room nights
-   Website conversion = confirmed direct bookings / eligible booking
    journey sessions
-   Room turnaround = checkout dirty event to inspected-ready event
-   Average check = net settled restaurant sales / closed guest checks
-   Payment variance = actual cashier tender minus expected tender by
    shift and currency

KPI inclusion rules must be approved by the hotel. Reports must expose
date basis, tax treatment, cancellation handling, complimentary handling
and currency assumptions.
