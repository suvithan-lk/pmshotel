# Twelve-Week Roadmap and Release Control

## Week 1

Discovery, operating policy, architecture, environments and backlog.

Exit: approved MVP, process owners, architecture baseline and delivery
pipeline.

## Week 2

Identity, permissions, property, rooms, outlets, taxes and business
date.

Exit: scoped users can enter validated hotel configuration.

## Week 3

Availability, rate plans, restrictions, quote and concurrency.

Exit: tested sellable room search with complete commercial terms.

## Week 4

Reservations, guests, sources, confirmation, change and cancellation.

Exit: staff booking lifecycle with inventory integrity.

## Week 5

Front desk, room assignment, check-in, stay and room move.

Exit: arrival through active stay demonstrated.

## Week 6

Folio, accommodation posting, payment references, checkout and
housekeeping.

Exit: stay charges and departure workflow reconcile.

## Week 7

Restaurant menu, orders, modifiers, KOT and kitchen states.

Exit: table and room-service order reaches kitchen correctly.

## Week 8

POS settlement, room charge, shift close and outlet reports.

Exit: POS bill settles and verified room charge posts once.

## Week 9

Rebranded website, content, analytics and direct booking.

Exit: mobile website search to confirmation demonstrated.

## Week 10

Migration rehearsal, reporting, performance, security and operations
hardening.

Exit: reconciled rehearsal and nonfunctional evidence.

## Week 11

UAT, training, cutover and recovery rehearsal.

Exit: named owners sign critical scenarios and run runbooks.

## Week 12

Production cutover, go live, command center and stabilization.

Exit: controlled operations with monitoring and daily reconciliation.

## Release gates

A dependency that threatens operational safety, reconciliation, data
integrity, training or recovery moves to a later phase unless the
steering group formally changes date/resources.

## Later phases

-   Stabilization: defect burn-down, operational tuning, reconciliation
    and adoption.
-   Phase 2: certified channels, deeper facilities, inventory, CRM, AI
    assistance and accounting exports.
-   Phase 3: procurement, advanced finance, revenue optimization,
    loyalty and richer automation.
-   Expansion: multi-property, mobile depth and ecosystem integrations.
